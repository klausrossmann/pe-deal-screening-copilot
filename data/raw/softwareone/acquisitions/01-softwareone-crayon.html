  3. SoftwareOne and Crayon

2 July 2025

\n

SoftwareOne and Crayon have joined forces, combining two leading global providers of software and cloud solutions. This step marks a new chapter in our growth journey — bringing together two trusted organizations, with around 13,000 dedicated employees across 70 countries, based on complementary strengths, shared values, and a common goal: to deliver even greater value for clients, partners, and shareholders.

A new chapter in innovation: united for growth

We are confident that this next chapter will unlock even more possibilities in how we engage with you and continue to deliver exceptional outcomes.

Melissa Mulholland and Raphael Erb

Co-CEOs

Read the letter from our Co-CEOs, Raphael Erb and Melissa Mulholland, as they share their vision for the future of SoftwareOne and Crayon — together shaping a stronger, smarter digital services powerhouse.
