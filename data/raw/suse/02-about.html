At SUSE, we believe that open source is the very best way to drive innovation, and has been for decades.  When we collaborate with partners and communities, we give customers the agility to tackle innovation challenges today and the freedom to evolve their strategy and solutions tomorrow. Open is an ethos that influences everything we do.
