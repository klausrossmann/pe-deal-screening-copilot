We design working environments with a future

From vision to reality

As a trendsetter and innovator in the workforce management market, we have a clear vision of how we can change the world of work for the better and create value for our customers. This vision of a human economy drives us forward and motivates us.

We are organizing the working world of the 21st century

The newspaper DIE WELT counts us among the key players that are organizing the working worlds of the 21st century. We are mentioned in the same breath along with companies like Adobe, ADP and Microsoft. This is not only a matter of pride, but also an incentive for us to bring our vision to life day in, day out. 

The ATOSS vision becomes reality. DIE WELT ranks us among the most important companies organizing work in the 21st century.

Our vision

R:evolution in working life

We are empowering the revolution in working environments to the benefit of companies, employees and society. We are paving the way to working lives that are more creative, intelligent and more human. At the same time, we are revolutionizing the interaction of cost efficiency and humanity.

Our mission

R:evolution in Staff Efficiency

Every day we are improving our customers’ efficiency, supporting their innovative capabilities, while enhancing employee motivation and satisfaction. Our achievements are convincing more and more people that workforce management is the way to go.

Our employees excel through their skills, sense of personal responsibility, team spirit and the pleasure they derive from their meaningful work. We collaborate closely and openly with our customers and business partners, and grasp the growth opportunities presenting themselves. We work to realize our vision, ensure profitability and secure the future of our customers, employees and investors.
