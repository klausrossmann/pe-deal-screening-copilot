Comprehensive functionality and leading-edge technology

ATOSS solutions impress with state-of-the-art web technology, modern interaction design and their puristic look – in connection with top-notch functionality. And we are continuously developing and refining our software. This entails regular cycles in which hundreds of innovations every year make our solutions even stronger. Our research has a clear focus on technology, AI and self-learning systems. For even more intelligent workforce management and the future-viable organization of working time. 

High investment security through cloud solutions 

All ATOSS product suites are available in the cloud. This means maximum cost transparency, while sparing IT resources, eliminating hardware costs and providing a long-term secure solution. After all, our experts take care of the hosting and management of the workforce management for you. 

Greater freedom of action thanks to parameterization

The great advantage of our solutions: They are flexibly scalable and parameterizable. Adaptations are implemented without programming. New requirements, expansion or internationalization of your business model? No problem – your workforce management solution simply grows along with your challenges. 

Mapping of all compliance requirements 

ATOSS solutions are able to map laws, tariffs and regulations in their standard versions. Nation-wide and across the globe. A crucial aspect when it comes to compliance. Consequently, if your business makes these demands you can count on an internationally consistent solution that is locally compliant. 

Open for an integrated system landscape

By way of open interfaces, our solutions interlink with upstream and downstream systems, such as ERP systems, for example. That’s workforce management without system boundaries. Our interfaces to HR systems such as SAP SuccessFactors and SAP HCM PT are prime examples.
